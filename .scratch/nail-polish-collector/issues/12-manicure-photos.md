# 12: Manicure photos: upload, basic edit, visibility

**What to build:** A User creates a Manicure by uploading up to 4 photos (10 MB each, resized, EXIF stripped, stored in Spaces behind the CDN), rotates them, adds a date and caption, saves, and makes it public or private. Account default is public with a per-Manicure override, effective immediately. The User's profile lists their Manicures. Soft cap of 500 photos per User.
